# 400-word Reflection Template

Write this in your own words after you run and test the project.

Cover:
1. The campus problem and who benefits.
2. How the specification shaped the implementation.
3. What worked and what failed during extraction and matching.
4. What the tests and red-team inputs revealed.
5. Why the tool must not decide ownership.
6. Limitations of the local fallback and the LLM.
7. What you would improve next.

Do not submit this template unchanged as your reflection. Include your actual test results and your own learning.
